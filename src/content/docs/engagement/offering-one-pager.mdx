## When to use this

Hand this to a prospective customer in the very first conversation — pre-sales, account team intro, or unsolicited inbound. It explains *what* you sell, *who* it is for, *what they get*, and *how long it takes*, in a single page.

It is also the artefact your sellers and PDM (Partner Development Manager) use to position you to Microsoft's field for co-sell motions tied to the Azure AI workload.

## Inputs you need from the customer

The one-pager is generic; it does not require customer-specific inputs to send. However, before tailoring the cover slide / cover page for a specific account, capture:

| # | Input | Source | Format |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Account name + industry | CRM | Text |
| 2 | Stated AI ambition or use case | Discovery call notes | Text |
| 3 | Existing Azure footprint signal (yes/no) | PDM / Azure usage data | Boolean |
| 4 | Solutions Partner designation status of *your* org | Partner Center | Text |

## Step-by-step

Use the downloadable PowerPoint template and replace the highlighted placeholders.

<Steps>

1. **Set the headline value proposition.** One sentence: who you help, to do what, with what Azure AI services. Avoid feature lists.

2. **List the three packages.** Standard menu: *Envisioning Workshop (1 week)*, *RAG Pilot on Azure OpenAI / AI Foundry (4 weeks)*, *Production AI App on AKS or Azure Container Apps (8–12 weeks)*. Adjust durations to your delivery capacity.

3. **State outcomes per package**, not activities. Example: "Live RAG chatbot grounded on customer documents, deployed to Azure App Service, with measured answer quality &gt; 80% on a 50-question eval set."

4. **List the AI Apps Azure service stack** the customer should expect to see: Azure OpenAI in Azure AI Foundry, Azure AI Search, Azure Container Apps or AKS or App Service, Azure Cosmos DB or Azure SQL, Azure Monitor + Application Insights, Microsoft Entra ID.

5. **Add the responsible-AI commitment.** Reference your Responsible AI policy (or Microsoft's Responsible AI Standard if you piggyback). This is required for credible AI Apps sales.

6. **Add proof points.** Up to three anonymised reference customers — same anonymisation rules as the audit (Customer A / B / C).

7. **Add the CTA.** Single CTA: "Book a 60-minute envisioning workshop." Include the named contact.

</Steps>

## Output: customer-ready deliverable

A single PowerPoint slide (16:9) or a one-page PDF, branded with your logo, that the seller can email after the first conversation.
