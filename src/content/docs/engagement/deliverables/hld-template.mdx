## When to use this

Produce one HLD per engagement, immediately after the discovery workshop. The HLD is the customer's first signable architectural artefact and the basis for the LLD and the WAF assessment.

## Inputs you need from the customer

| # | Input | Source | Format |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Discovery report (use case, data, RAI guardrails) | Discovery workshop | Document |
| 2 | Non-functional requirements (SLO, throughput, cost) | Customer | Document |
| 3 | Identity & networking constraints | Customer | Document |
| 4 | Compliance scope (e.g. ISO 27001, GDPR, HIPAA) | Customer compliance | Document |

## Document structure

The downloadable template ships with these sections — keep them all, even if some are short, so the HLD shape is consistent across engagements.

<Steps>

1. **Cover & document control** — version, authors, reviewers, sign-off block.

2. **Executive summary** — the use case in one paragraph, the chosen reference architecture pattern, the headline cost and timeline.

3. **Business context & success metrics** — what the AI app will measurably change.

4. **Scope** — in / out / deferred. Be explicit about what *isn't* in the engagement.

5. **Solution architecture** — block diagram, narrative, chosen Azure services, justification for each service vs. alternatives.

6. **Data architecture** — sources, classification, flows, retention, residency.

7. **Identity & access** — Entra ID model, managed identities, RBAC scopes, conditional access posture.

8. **Networking** — public vs. private, private endpoints, egress controls, DNS, WAF.

9. **Responsible AI** — harms identification, mitigations (system prompt, content filters, abuse monitoring), human-in-the-loop checkpoints, transparency notes.

10. **Non-functional requirements** — availability, latency, throughput, RPO / RTO, scalability strategy, cost envelope.

11. **Operations** — observability, alerting, on-call, runbook reference, change control for prompts and models.

12. **Risks & assumptions** — register with owner and mitigation.

13. **Phased delivery plan** — milestones, gates, deliverables per phase.

</Steps>

## Output: customer-ready deliverable

A signed HLD in PDF, version-controlled in the engagement folder, with a one-page summary slide that the customer sponsor can take to their steerco.
