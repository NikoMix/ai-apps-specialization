## When to use this

Produce one Solution Architecture Design per engagement immediately after discovery. It is the first signable architecture artefact for the customer and the baseline for the Detailed Technical Design and the Well-Architected Framework assessment.

## Inputs you need from the customer

| # | Input | Source | Format |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Discovery report with use case, data, and responsible-AI guardrails | Discovery workshop | Document |
| 2 | Non-functional requirements including Service Level Objective targets, throughput, and cost envelope | Customer | Document |
| 3 | Identity and networking constraints | Customer | Document |
| 4 | Compliance scope such as ISO 27001, GDPR, or HIPAA | Customer compliance | Document |

## Document structure

<Steps>
1. **Cover and document control** — version, authors, reviewers, and sign-off block.
2. **Executive summary** — business outcome, chosen pattern, delivery approach, and headline costs.
3. **Business context and success metrics** — what the AI application changes and how value is measured.
4. **Scope and assumptions** — in-scope, out-of-scope, and deferred work.
5. **Solution architecture** — Azure services, integration pattern, security posture, and justification.
6. **Data architecture** — sources, classification, retention, residency, and grounding path.
7. **Responsible AI approach** — harms, mitigations, human review, and transparency.
8. **Operations and support model** — observability, release controls, and incident ownership.
9. **Risks and phased plan** — risks, dependencies, milestones, and gate decisions.
</Steps>

## Output: customer-ready deliverable

A signed Solution Architecture Design in PDF, stored with the engagement record and linked to the supporting architecture decisions and assessments.
