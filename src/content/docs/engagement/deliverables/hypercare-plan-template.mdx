## When to use this

Agree the hypercare plan in the LLD, finalise it the week before go-live, execute it for a fixed window after go-live (typically 4–8 weeks), and exit on a pre-agreed gate. Hypercare is *not* "we'll be around if you need us" — it is a structured, time-boxed, exit-criteria-driven plan.

## Inputs you need from the customer

| # | Input | Source | Format |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Signed Definition of Done acceptance criteria | Closeout planning | Document |
| 2 | Customer on-call rota for the hypercare window | Customer | Document |
| 3 | SLO targets the workload must hold for exit | Customer | Numeric |
| 4 | Named hypercare sponsor on the customer side | Customer | Contact |

## Document structure

<Steps>

1. **Duration & window** — start date, end date, daily working hours, time zone, weekend coverage.

2. **Team & rota** — your engineers on call, customer engineers shadowing, escalation tree, Microsoft support plan and severity routing.

3. **Daily cadence** — 15-minute standup, KPI snapshot review, action log update.

4. **Weekly cadence** — 1-hour steerco with customer sponsor: KPIs vs. SLO, open incidents, change requests, exit-gate readiness.

5. **KPIs to track**

   - Availability vs. SLO
   - p95 latency vs. SLO
   - RAG / model quality on the eval set (rolling)
   - Content-filter / abuse-monitor signals
   - Cost vs. budget
   - Incident count by severity

6. **Incident handling** — same flow as the runbook but with response-time SLAs tightened for the hypercare window.

7. **Change control** — restricted in hypercare. Only blocking fixes; no new features. Document the policy and exceptions process.

8. **Exit gate** — explicit, numerical, signed. Typical: 14 consecutive days without a Sev-1, all Sev-2s closed, SLOs met, customer on-call has handled at least one real incident unaided.

9. **Handover at exit** — formal sign-off, transition to BAU support contract (or none), retrospective with lessons learned filed back into this repo.

</Steps>

## Output: customer-ready deliverable

A signed hypercare plan with weekly KPI reports filed against the engagement, and a signed exit certificate that triggers final invoice and BAU transition.
