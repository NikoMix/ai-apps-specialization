## What is the AI Applications on Microsoft Azure Advanced Specialization?

The **AI Applications on Microsoft Azure Advanced Specialization** is a Microsoft Partner credential that validates deep technical expertise in designing, building, and deploying AI-powered applications on Azure. It signals to customers and Microsoft that your organisation:

- Has **proven delivery capability** for AI and cloud-native workloads
- Generates **measurable Azure consumed revenue** across AI, application platform, and data services
- Employs **certified professionals** across the relevant Azure disciplines
- Has passed a rigorous **independent third-party audit** validating your processes and customer outcomes

<Aside type="tip">
  Partners who hold Advanced Specializations typically appear higher in the Microsoft Partner Finder,
  qualify for additional co-sell benefits, and gain access to Microsoft-funded marketing programmes.
</Aside>

## Why pursue this specialization?

| Benefit | Details |
|---|---|
| **Differentiation** | Stand out in a crowded market with a verifiable, audited credential |
| **Co-sell eligibility** | Unlock priority co-sell referrals from Microsoft field sellers |
| **Marketing assets** | Use the specialization badge and Microsoft-approved marketing collateral |
| **Partner Finder visibility** | Appear in customer-facing searches filtered by specialization |
| **Customer confidence** | Demonstrate credibility backed by an independent audit |

## How this guide helps

This repository is structured as an **engagement toolkit**. Each page maps to a specific audit control and contains:

1. **What the auditor checks** — exact criteria from the audit checklist
2. **Required evidence** — a structured checklist of accepted document types
3. **Evidence status table** — assign owners, set deadlines, and track status
4. **Tips** — practical advice for gathering and formatting evidence

Use this guide alongside your Microsoft Partner Center portal and your internal project management tooling.
