## What the Auditor Checks

The auditor verifies that your organisation has **at least five individuals** holding the required Microsoft certifications, with each of the four mandatory certifications held by at least one individual.

**Typical questions:**
- Can you provide verification links or transcripts for each certified individual?
- Are all certifications currently active (not expired)?
- Do the individuals work for your organisation (not contractors)?

---

## Required Certifications

| Certification | Exam Code | Minimum Holders | Renewal |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Azure Developer Associate** | AZ-204 | 1 | Annual renewal exam |
| **DevOps Engineer Expert** | AZ-400 | 1 | Annual renewal exam |
| **Azure AI Engineer Associate** | AI-102 | 1 | Annual renewal exam |
| **Azure Cosmos DB Developer Specialty** | DP-420 | 1 | Annual renewal exam |

<Aside>
  The total of **five individuals** can be any combination. For example:
  - 4 individuals each holding one unique certification + 1 individual holding any of the four = 5 ✅
  - 1 individual holding all 4 + 4 individuals each holding 1 = 5 ✅ (but the multi-cert holder counts once)
  - 5 individuals each holding 1 certification (with all 4 covered) = 5 ✅
</Aside>

## Certification Validity

| Certification | Exam | Renewal Method | Renewal Window |
|---|---|---|---|
| Azure Developer Associate | AZ-204 | Free online renewal assessment on Microsoft Learn | 6 months before expiry |
| DevOps Engineer Expert | AZ-400 | Free online renewal assessment | 6 months before expiry |
| Azure AI Engineer Associate | AI-102 | Free online renewal assessment | 6 months before expiry |
| Azure Cosmos DB Developer | DP-420 | Free online renewal assessment | 6 months before expiry |

<Aside type="caution">
  Certifications must be **active at the time of audit**. Check expiry dates now and schedule
  renewal assessments for anyone expiring within 3 months.
</Aside>

## Common Gaps

| Gap | Remediation |
|---|---|
| Missing DP-420 (Cosmos DB) | This is the least common cert — prioritise scheduling this exam for an eligible team member |
| Certifications held by contractors, not employees | Confirm employment status; some auditors accept long-term contractors with a letter from HR |
| Expired certifications | Complete the free Microsoft Learn renewal assessment immediately |
| Transcripts set to private | Instruct individuals to set their Learn profile to public or export a PDF transcript |
