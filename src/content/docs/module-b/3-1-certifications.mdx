## What the Auditor Checks

The auditor confirms that the organisation has the required Microsoft-certified talent in-house and that each mandatory certification is currently active. They may also test whether the certified people are actually mapped to the AI delivery practice rather than sitting elsewhere in the business.

Good evidence is a clean certification matrix backed by Microsoft Learn transcripts or verification links, plus an ownership view that shows how those people support sales, design, build, and operations.

## Required Evidence Checklist

| # | Evidence Item | Accepted Formats | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Microsoft Learn transcript or public verification link for each individual | URL, PDF | ⬜ |
| 2 | Certification matrix mapping people, role, certification, issue date, and expiry date | Excel, PDF | ⬜ |
| 3 | Employment confirmation for each individual if requested | HR letter, contract summary | ⬜ |
| 4 | Coverage summary proving each required certification is present and at least five people are counted | Excel, PDF | ⬜ |

## Implementation Guidance

### Build the certification matrix

- List every qualifying person once, even if they hold several certifications, then map which requirement each certification satisfies.
- Capture issue date, expiry date, and the direct evidence link in the same worksheet.
- Mark any person whose certification expires within the next 90 days and schedule renewal immediately.

### Connect certifications to delivery

- Identify which certified people play architectural, engineering, DevOps, and AI workload roles in customer engagements.
- Use the same names across the organisation chart, case studies, and key personnel list so the auditor can trace capability.
- Keep a shadow plan for coverage if one person is on leave or leaves the business close to audit.

### Close gaps quickly

- Prioritise DP-420 early because it is often the least common requirement in AI-focused teams.
- Use Microsoft Learn training paths and internal lab exercises as part of the readiness plan.
- Freeze evidence as PDF right after checking transcript visibility so permissions do not drift before audit.
