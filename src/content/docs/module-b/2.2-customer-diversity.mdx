## What the Auditor Checks

The auditor verifies that your organisation has **at least three unique customers** contributing to ACR in the last three months from the eligible strategic pillars — demonstrating breadth of customer reach, not just depth with a single client.

**Typical questions:**
- How many unique customers contribute to your qualifying ACR?
- Are all three using eligible association types (DPOR, PAL, CSP)?
- Can you demonstrate diversity across industries or workload types?

<Aside>
  **Eligible Association Types:**
  - Digital Partner of Record (DPOR)
  - Partner Admin Link (PAL)
  - Cloud Solution Provider (CSP)
</Aside>

---

## Eligible Strategic Pillars

A customer counts toward the diversity requirement if they contribute ACR in **any** of the following pillars:

| Module | Eligible Pillars |
|---|---|
| AI | Rest of Azure AI, Azure OpenAI, 3P GPU |
| App Platform | App Platform Services, Container Services, Serverless and Supporting App Services, Integration Services, Developer Services and Platform, GitHub Copilot |
| Data | Modern DB, MySQL and PostgreSQL PaaS, Azure SQL Core, Fabric F SKU |

## How to Identify Unique Customer Contributions

1. In Partner Center → Insights → Azure Revenue, switch to the **"By Customer"** view
2. Filter by the last 3 months
3. Identify customers contributing ACR in the eligible service categories
4. Cross-reference with your DPOR/PAL/CSP association records

<Aside type="caution">
  If a customer has revenue under multiple association types, they still count as **one unique
  customer**. Likewise, a customer contributing across multiple pillars counts as one.
</Aside>

## Common Gaps

| Gap | Remediation |
|---|---|
| Only 1–2 customers meet the association criteria | Urgently establish DPOR or PAL links with additional customers where you deliver services |
| ACR concentrated in one customer | Cannot be resolved short-term; plan to diversify customer base for the next ACR window |
| Customer association records not exportable | Take screenshots from Partner Center's customer association views; annotate with customer IDs |
| Revenue from ineligible services | Review the full eligible service list and ensure customer workloads are correctly categorised |
