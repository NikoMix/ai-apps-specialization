## What the Auditor Checks

The auditor checks that the partner has sustained qualifying Azure Consumed Revenue (ACR) over the trailing three-month window in each required pillar: AI services, application platform, and data platform. They are not looking for a narrative estimate; they want Partner Center exports that reconcile to the required thresholds.

The strongest submission shows the raw Partner Center extract, an annotated pillar mapping, and an internal summary that explains how services were bucketed and which partner association types apply to the revenue.

## Required Evidence Checklist

| # | Evidence Item | Accepted Formats | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Partner Center revenue export covering the latest complete three-month window | Excel, PDF | ⬜ |
| 2 | Pillar mapping showing which services were counted in AI, application platform, and data platform | Excel, PDF | ⬜ |
| 3 | Evidence of eligible partner association types such as Digital Partner of Record, Partner Admin Link, or Cloud Solution Provider | PDF, screenshot | ⬜ |
| 4 | Internal threshold tracker showing each pillar against the minimum target | Excel, PDF | ⬜ |
| 5 | Partner Development Manager confirmation or escalation email if figures are close to the threshold | Email, PDF | ⬜ |

## Implementation Guidance

### Build the evidence pack

- Export the last three full months from Partner Center and freeze the download date in the file name.
- Annotate the export so the auditor can see which rows contribute to AI, app platform, and data platform.
- Keep the raw extract unedited and store the annotation in a separate workbook or PDF.

### Validate the numbers

- Check that each pillar clears the minimum independently; one over-performing pillar cannot compensate for another.
- Confirm that the association type behind the revenue is eligible and still active in the audit period.
- If you are close to the threshold, request written confirmation from your Partner Development Manager before the audit kickoff.

### Operationalise the control

- Track Azure Consumed Revenue weekly during the lead-up to audit so you do not discover a shortfall late.
- Map high-value customer workloads to the Azure services they consume so finance, sales, and delivery speak the same language.
- Treat this control as a rolling metric, not a one-time audit task, because the specialization must be maintained.
