## Overview

The Advanced Specialization audit is conducted by a **Microsoft-approved third-party audit firm**. The audit is performed remotely (video call + document review) and typically takes **2–4 weeks** from first contact to result, depending on evidence readiness.

<Aside type="tip">
  Partners who prepare their evidence package in advance (using this guide) consistently report faster
  audit cycles and fewer remediation rounds. Aim to have all evidence collected **before** requesting
  the audit.
</Aside>

---

## Audit Phases

<Steps>

1. ### Pre-Qualification Confirmation

   Before requesting the audit, confirm you meet all four [pre-qualification requirements](../requirements/).

   **Actions:**
   - Run the Quick Readiness Checklist on the Requirements page
   - Confirm ACR figures with your PDM (Insights data can lag by 2–4 weeks)
   - Ensure all certifications are active (not expired)

2. ### Audit Request via Partner Center

   Navigate to **Partner Center → Benefits → Advanced Specializations** and request the audit for _AI Applications on Microsoft Azure_.

   **What happens next:**
   - Microsoft routes your request to the assigned audit firm
   - You receive an introductory email within 5–10 business days
   - A kickoff call is scheduled with your assigned auditor

3. ### Kickoff Call with Auditor

   The kickoff call is typically 30–60 minutes. Expect the auditor to:

   - Introduce the audit process and timeline
   - Confirm which controls will be assessed (Module A + Module B)
   - Agree on an evidence submission format (SharePoint, email, or their portal)
   - Set a deadline for evidence submission

   **Tip:** Bring your project lead, technical lead, and compliance/quality lead to this call.

4. ### Evidence Collection & Submission

   Use the [Evidence Tracker](../evidence-tracker/) to manage collection across all controls.

   <Tabs>
     <TabItem label="Module A – General">
       | Control | Key Evidence |
       |---|---|
       | [1.1 Organisational Data](../module-a/1-1-organizational-data/) | Articles of incorporation, org chart |
       | [1.2 Financial Documentation](../module-a/1-2-financial-documentation/) | Financial statements, professional insurance |
       | [2.1 Service Delivery Methodology](../module-a/2-1-service-delivery-methodology/) | Delivery methodology docs, project artefacts |
       | [2.2 Quality Management](../module-a/2-2-quality-management/) | QMS policy, CSAT process, escalation procedure |
       | [3.1 Customer Satisfaction](../module-a/3-1-customer-satisfaction/) | CSAT survey results, NPS data, references |
       | [3.2 Complaint Handling](../module-a/3-2-complaint-handling/) | Complaint logs, resolution records |
       | [3.3 Security & Privacy](../module-a/3-3-security-privacy/) | Data protection policy, GDPR evidence |
     </TabItem>
     <TabItem label="Module B – AI Apps">
       | Control | Key Evidence |
       |---|---|
       | [1.1 Azure AI Implementation](../module-b/1-1-azure-ai-implementation/) | Architecture diagrams, solution delivery examples |
       | [2.1 ACR Performance](../module-b/2-1-acr-performance/) | Partner Center ACR reports |
       | [2.2 Customer Diversity](../module-b/2-2-customer-diversity/) | Customer list with ACR associations |

       | [4.2 Partner Onboarding](../module-b/4-2-partner-onboarding/) | Customer onboarding assets, delivery templates |
     </TabItem>
   </Tabs>

5. ### Auditor Review

   The auditor reviews your submitted evidence against the checklist and may request clarifications or additional documents. Typical review duration: **5–10 business days**.

   - Respond to queries promptly (target &lt; 2 business days)
   - Assign a single point of contact for auditor communication
   - Keep evidence version-controlled (use dated filenames)

6. ### Audit Interview (if required)

   Some auditors request a short video call to walk through evidence for specific controls. Prepare your technical and delivery leads to discuss:

   - How you deliver AI solutions on Azure
   - Your quality management and complaint handling processes
   - Real customer engagement examples (anonymised if necessary)

7. ### Remediation (if applicable)

   If the auditor identifies gaps, they provide a **remediation report** listing the specific controls and missing evidence.

   <Aside type="caution">
     You typically have **30 days** to address remediation findings. Treat this as a priority — delays
     can result in needing to restart the audit cycle.
   </Aside>

   - Address each finding systematically using the Module A/B pages in this guide
   - Resubmit only the affected controls unless instructed otherwise

8. ### Audit Pass & Specialization Award

   Once the auditor confirms a pass, Microsoft Partner Center is updated within **10–15 business days**. You will see the specialization badge appear on your profile.

   **Next steps after passing:**
   - Download the specialization badge from Partner Center
   - Update your marketing materials and website
   - Notify your PDM to unlock co-sell and marketing benefits

</Steps>

## Timeline Summary

| Phase | Typical Duration |
|---|---|
| Pre-qualification confirmation | 1–2 weeks |
| Audit request to auditor assignment | 5–10 business days |
| Evidence collection (prepared) | 1–2 weeks |
| Evidence collection (unprepared) | 4–8 weeks |
| Auditor review | 5–10 business days |
| Remediation (if needed) | Up to 30 days |
| Specialization badge publication | 10–15 business days |
| **Total (well-prepared partner)** | **~6–8 weeks** |
