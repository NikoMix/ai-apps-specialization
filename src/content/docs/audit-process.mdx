## Overview

The audit is conducted by a Microsoft-approved third-party firm and usually runs remotely across document review plus one or more calls. Well-prepared partners typically move from audit request to outcome faster because evidence is already collected, labelled, and internally reviewed.

## Audit Phases

<Steps>
1. **Pre-qualification confirmation** — confirm designations, Azure Consumed Revenue, certifications, and internal evidence ownership.
2. **Audit request** — submit through Partner Center and wait for the assigned auditor to make contact.
3. **Kickoff call** — confirm evidence channels, timeline, and who will answer technical follow-up questions.
4. **Evidence submission** — package Module A and Module B evidence with a clean file index and named owners.
5. **Auditor review** — answer clarification questions quickly and keep a dated change log for anything resubmitted.
6. **Interview or walkthrough** — be ready to explain delivery methodology, Azure AI implementations, and evidence provenance.
7. **Remediation if needed** — address only the controls in scope, but update the evidence index so the package remains coherent.
8. **Award and post-pass actions** — confirm the badge appears in Partner Center and capture lessons learned into inner-source assets.
</Steps>

## Timeline summary

| Phase | Typical Duration |
| --- | --- |
| Pre-qualification confirmation | 1-2 weeks |
| Audit request to auditor assignment | 5-10 business days |
| Evidence collection when prepared | 1-2 weeks |
| Evidence collection when unprepared | 4-8 weeks |
| Auditor review | 5-10 business days |
| Remediation if required | Up to 30 days |
| Badge publication after pass | 10-15 business days |

## Key contacts

| Role | Responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| Partner Development Manager | Qualification questions and Azure Consumed Revenue confirmation |
| Audit firm | Audit schedule, clarification requests, and formal outcome |
| Internal engagement lead | Evidence coordination and response management |
| Technical lead | Module B technical evidence and architecture walkthroughs |
| Compliance or quality lead | Module A evidence, controls, and policy ownership |

<Aside type="tip">
  Aim to have a single evidence owner for every control before the kickoff call. That one change removes most of the confusion that causes slow remediation cycles.
</Aside>
