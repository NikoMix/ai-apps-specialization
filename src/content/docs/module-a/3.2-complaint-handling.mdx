## What the Auditor Checks

The auditor verifies that your organisation has a **formal process for receiving, recording, and resolving customer complaints**, and that this process has been applied in practice.

**Typical questions:**
- Is there a documented complaint handling procedure?
- How are complaints logged and tracked?
- Can you show evidence that a complaint was received and resolved?

---

## Required Evidence Checklist

| # | Evidence Item | Accepted Formats | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Complaint handling procedure** | PDF, Word | ⬜ |
| 2 | **Complaint register / log** (can be a template with anonymised entries) | Excel, PDF | ⬜ |
| 3 | **At least one complaint case** showing receipt, handling, and resolution | PDF, Email thread, Word | ⬜ |
| 4 | **Root cause analysis** or corrective action for at least one complaint | PDF, Word | ⬜ |

<Aside type="tip">
  If your organisation genuinely has zero complaints, explain how that is tracked (e.g., no entries
  in the complaint register). Some auditors may accept this with a signed declaration. However, having
  one real (resolved) complaint example often provides stronger evidence of a working process.
</Aside>

## Evidence Guidance

### Complaint Handling Procedure

The procedure must include:

1. **Definition of a complaint** — what qualifies as a complaint vs. service request
2. **How to raise a complaint** — contact details, form, email address
3. **Acknowledgement SLA** — e.g., within 1 business day
4. **Investigation process** — who investigates, how long it takes
5. **Resolution SLA** — e.g., within 5 business days for Level 1
6. **Escalation path** — what happens if unresolved (aligned with A.2.2 escalation procedure)
7. **Recording** — where complaints are logged and by whom
8. **Root cause and corrective action** — how systemic issues are prevented

### Complaint Register

A simple spreadsheet is sufficient. Suggested columns:

| ID | Date Received | Customer | Description | Severity | Owner | Status | Resolution Date | Root Cause | Corrective Action |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Anonymise customer names before submitting to the auditor.

### Case Evidence

For each case, provide:
- The original complaint (email or form submission, anonymised)
- Acknowledgement sent to the customer
- Resolution summary
- Any corrective actions taken

## Common Gaps

| Gap | Remediation |
|---|---|
| Complaint handling is part of an informal escalation email chain | Formalise the process into a 1-page document; backfill the register with historical entries |
| No complaint register exists | Create a register now and retrospectively log any issues from memory, with best-effort dates |
| Complaints were handled but not logged | Interview delivery leads and recreate log entries with a note that records are retrospectively captured |
| Root cause analysis not performed | Document a retrospective root cause for the most recent complaint |
