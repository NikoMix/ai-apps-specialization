## What the Auditor Checks

The auditor verifies that your organisation uses a documented, repeatable delivery approach rather than relying on tribal knowledge. They also expect proof that the methodology is used in live customer work through real artefacts and approval points.

## Required Evidence Checklist

| # | Evidence Item | Accepted Formats | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Documented delivery methodology or playbook | PDF, Word | ⬜ |
| 2 | Project charter or statement of work template | PDF, Word | ⬜ |
| 3 | Kickoff artefact from a real engagement, anonymised if needed | PDF, PowerPoint | ⬜ |
| 4 | Closure or handover artefact from a real engagement | PDF, Word | ⬜ |
| 5 | Risk register or issue log template | PDF, Excel, Word | ⬜ |
| 6 | Responsibility Assignment Matrix (RACI) or roles template | PDF, Excel, Word | ⬜ |

## Quick Guidance

- Show phase gates clearly: discovery, design, build, test, deploy, and handover.
- Include owners and approvers for each stage so governance is visible to the auditor.
- Use one or two anonymised customer artefacts to prove the method is not only theoretical.
- Version the methodology document and give it an owner such as Head of Delivery.
- Keep your template names consistent across the audit package and engagement playbook.
