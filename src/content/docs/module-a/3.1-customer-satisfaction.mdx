## What the Auditor Checks

The auditor verifies that your organisation has **demonstrated customer satisfaction outcomes** — not just a process for measuring satisfaction, but actual data showing customers are satisfied with your services.

**Typical questions:**
- What are your CSAT/NPS scores for the last 12 months?
- Can you provide customer references who would vouch for your delivery quality?
- How do you act on customer feedback?

<Aside>
  This control is distinct from A.2.2 (Quality Management). A.2.2 covers the *process* for measuring
  satisfaction; A.3.1 covers the *outcomes* — the actual data and reference customers.
</Aside>

## Required Evidence Checklist

| # | Evidence Item | Accepted Formats | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **CSAT/NPS results** for the last 12 months (aggregated or per project) | PDF, Excel | ⬜ |
| 2 | **At least two customer reference summaries** (anonymised acceptable) | PDF, Word | ⬜ |
| 3 | **Customer testimonials or quotes** (at least 2) | PDF, Word, Email | ⬜ |
| 4 | **Evidence of action taken on low CSAT scores** (at least 1 example) | PDF, Email thread, Word | ⬜ |

<Aside type="tip">
  For the Module B ACR customer diversity requirement (B.2.2), consider reusing references here —
  customers who contributed ACR and gave positive CSAT scores are strong dual evidence.
</Aside>

## Evidence Guidance

### CSAT / NPS Data

Present data as a summary table or chart. Minimum information:

| Metric | Last 12 Months |
|---|---|
| Average CSAT score | e.g., 4.3 / 5 |
| Number of responses | e.g., 28 |
| NPS score | e.g., +42 |
| % responses scoring ≥4/5 | e.g., 89% |
| Lowest score received | e.g., 2/5 (see action log) |

### Customer Reference Summaries

For each reference, provide a short summary:

```
Customer: [Anonymised as "Customer A" or full name with consent]
Industry: Financial Services
Engagement: Azure OpenAI integration into customer service platform
Duration: 6 months
Outcome: Reduced call handling time by 35%, CSAT score 5/5
Contact available for auditor: Yes / No
```

### Testimonials

Written testimonials on customer letterhead carry the most weight. Email testimonials are also accepted. Ensure:
- Customer name or anonymised reference is clear
- Testimonial references the type of service delivered
- Date is visible

## Common Gaps

| Gap | Remediation |
|---|---|
| CSAT data exists but covers <6 months | Supplement with informal customer feedback emails as supporting evidence |
| No written testimonials | Request a short email testimonial from two satisfied customers — most clients are willing |
| Scores are high but no action evidence | Prepare a brief note documenting how one piece of feedback led to a process improvement |
| References are all from the same industry | Diversify by identifying customers across 2+ industries |
