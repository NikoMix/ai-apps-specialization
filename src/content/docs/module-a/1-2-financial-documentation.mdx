## What the Auditor Checks

The auditor verifies that your organisation is **financially viable** and holds appropriate **professional liability insurance** for the services it delivers.

**Typical questions:**
- Does the company have audited or reviewed financial statements?
- Is professional indemnity / errors & omissions insurance in place?
- Is there evidence of financial stability (no insolvency proceedings)?

| # | Evidence Item | Accepted Formats | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Financial statements** (most recent year, signed or audited) | PDF | ⬜ |
| 2 | **Professional indemnity / E&O insurance certificate** | PDF | ⬜ |
| 3 | **Public liability insurance certificate** (if applicable) | PDF | ⬜ |
| 4 | **No insolvency / bankruptcy declaration** (if required by auditor) | PDF, Signed letter | ⬜ |

<Aside type="caution">
  Insurance certificates must be **current** (not expired). Ensure the policy covers the period of
  the audit and renewal is tracked. Auditors may reject expired certificates.
</Aside>

## Evidence Guidance

### Financial Statements

Accepted documents in approximate preference order:

1. **Audited financial statements** — signed by an external auditor (highest credibility)
2. **Reviewed financial statements** — signed by an external accountant
3. **Management accounts** — internally prepared, signed by a director/CFO
4. **Filed accounts** from Companies House / equivalent registry

If your organisation is a subsidiary, the auditor may accept parent company financials if accompanied by a group structure document.

### Professional Indemnity Insurance

The certificate must show:
- Policy holder name (must match the legal entity name in A.1.1)
- Coverage amount
- Policy period (must be active)
- Type of coverage (professional indemnity / errors & omissions)

**Recommended minimum coverage:** £1M / €1M / $1M per claim (check with your auditor for jurisdiction-specific requirements).

### Redaction Policy

You may redact sensitive financial figures if they are not relevant to demonstrating viability. However, the **total revenue line** and **profit/loss** should remain visible to allow the auditor to assess financial health.

## Common Gaps

| Gap | Remediation |
|---|---|
| Insurance certificate names a trading name, not the legal entity | Request a certificate reissued in the legal entity name, or provide a DBA letter |
| Financial statements are >18 months old | Use management accounts for the current period, signed by a director |
| No insurance in place | Contact your broker immediately — this is a hard blocker |
