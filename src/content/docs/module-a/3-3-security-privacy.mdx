## What the Auditor Checks

The auditor verifies that the partner protects customer and personal data through documented security and privacy controls. They also want to see that staff understand those controls and that there is a response path for incidents or breaches.

## Required Evidence Checklist

| # | Evidence Item | Accepted Formats | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Information security policy | PDF, Word | ⬜ |
| 2 | Data protection or privacy policy | PDF, Word | ⬜ |
| 3 | Customer data processing agreement template | PDF, Word | ⬜ |
| 4 | Data breach response procedure | PDF, Word | ⬜ |
| 5 | Staff security awareness evidence | PDF, Excel | ⬜ |
| 6 | ISO 27001 or SOC 2 certificate, if available | PDF | ⬜ |

## Quick Guidance

- Lead with ISO 27001 or SOC 2 if you have it, then add the policy extracts that show scope.
- Make sure the privacy policy explains retention, deletion, and cross-border processing expectations.
- Show one place where staff acknowledge or complete annual security training.
- Keep the breach procedure short but explicit about containment, notification, and post-incident review.
- Use the same naming for data classifications here and in your engagement deliverables.
