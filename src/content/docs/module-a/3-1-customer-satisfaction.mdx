## What the Auditor Checks

The auditor looks for actual customer outcomes, not just a measurement process. They want to see recent satisfaction data, referenceable customers or testimonials, and proof that low scores led to action.

## Required Evidence Checklist

| # | Evidence Item | Accepted Formats | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Customer Satisfaction (CSAT) or NPS results for the last 12 months | PDF, Excel | ⬜ |
| 2 | At least two customer reference summaries | PDF, Word | ⬜ |
| 3 | At least two testimonials or customer quotes | PDF, Word, email | ⬜ |
| 4 | One example showing action taken on low feedback | PDF, email thread, Word | ⬜ |

## Quick Guidance

- Summarise your overall score, response volume, trend, and the lowest score received.
- Where possible, reuse customers who also contribute to Azure Consumed Revenue evidence so the audit pack stays consistent.
- Keep the reference summary short: customer context, engagement type, measurable outcome, and whether direct contact is available.
- A short improvement note based on poor feedback is stronger than claiming no issues ever occurred.
- If customer names must be hidden, anonymise them consistently across Module A and Module B.
