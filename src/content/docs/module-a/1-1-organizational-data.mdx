## What the Auditor Checks

The auditor confirms that the legal entity requesting the specialization is registered, active, and clearly accountable for delivery leadership. They also want to see an organisational structure that identifies executive sponsorship, delivery ownership, and customer-facing contacts.

## Required Evidence Checklist

| # | Evidence Item | Accepted Formats | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Certificate of incorporation or official registration extract | PDF | ⬜ |
| 2 | Current registered address evidence matching the legal entity | PDF, screenshot | ⬜ |
| 3 | Organisation chart showing delivery, security, and customer account roles | PDF, PowerPoint, Visio | ⬜ |
| 4 | Key personnel list with names, titles, and responsibilities | PDF, Word, Excel | ⬜ |

## Quick Guidance

- Use one current registry extract to cover legal entity name, registration number, and registered address.
- Show at least one executive role, one delivery lead, one technical lead, and one customer success or account role on the chart.
- Keep the key-personnel list aligned to the organisation chart so auditors can cross-reference names quickly.
- If your delivery team spans multiple countries, state the primary contracting entity explicitly.
- Export everything to PDF before submission so the evidence package is stable.
