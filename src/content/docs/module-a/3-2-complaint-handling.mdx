## What the Auditor Checks

The auditor checks that complaints can be received, logged, investigated, and resolved in a controlled way. Even if your complaint volume is low, they still need to see a documented process and evidence of how it is tracked.

## Required Evidence Checklist

| # | Evidence Item | Accepted Formats | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Complaint handling procedure | PDF, Word | ⬜ |
| 2 | Complaint register or log, template plus anonymised entries | Excel, PDF | ⬜ |
| 3 | At least one complaint case showing receipt, handling, and resolution | PDF, email thread, Word | ⬜ |
| 4 | Root cause analysis or corrective action record for one complaint | PDF, Word | ⬜ |

## Quick Guidance

- Define what counts as a complaint versus a service request so the auditor can see the threshold.
- Use a simple spreadsheet register if you do not have a service management tool.
- If you have had no complaints, document how that conclusion is reached and who monitors the register.
- Where a complaint did occur, include the acknowledgement, resolution, and the follow-up improvement.
- Align the escalation timings with the quality management evidence from A.2.2.
