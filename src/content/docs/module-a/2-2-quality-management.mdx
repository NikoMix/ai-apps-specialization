## What the Auditor Checks

The auditor wants evidence that quality is governed deliberately through policy, measurement, and escalation. That can be an ISO-certified quality management system or a lightweight internal framework, as long as it is documented and used.

## Required Evidence Checklist

| # | Evidence Item | Accepted Formats | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Quality management policy or equivalent quality framework | PDF, Word | ⬜ |
| 2 | Customer Satisfaction (CSAT) process document | PDF, Word | ⬜ |
| 3 | Sample Customer Satisfaction (CSAT) survey or form | PDF, screenshot, Word | ⬜ |
| 4 | Customer Satisfaction (CSAT) results summary for the last 12 months | PDF, Excel | ⬜ |
| 5 | Escalation procedure document | PDF, Word | ⬜ |
| 6 | An anonymised case showing the escalation process in action | PDF, Word | ⬜ |

## Quick Guidance

- If you have ISO 9001, lead with the certificate and the sections of the manual covering quality and escalation.
- Define who reviews quality metrics, how often they review them, and what triggers an action plan.
- Keep the Customer Satisfaction (CSAT) survey simple and show where the responses are stored.
- Use one sanitised incident or customer issue to prove the escalation path works in practice.
- Align the quality policy with complaint handling so the auditor sees one joined-up operating model.
