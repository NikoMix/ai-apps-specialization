## What the Auditor Checks

The auditor verifies that your organisation has a **formal quality management system (QMS)** — or equivalent documented quality processes — that includes customer satisfaction measurement and service escalation procedures.

**Typical questions:**
- Is there a documented quality policy?
- How is customer satisfaction measured and acted upon?
- What is the escalation process when service quality falls short?

## Evidence Guidance

### Quality Management Policy

The policy should cover:
- Quality objectives (measurable, e.g., "≥85% CSAT score on all projects")
- Management commitment statement
- Roles responsible for quality (Quality Manager, Delivery Lead, etc.)
- Review cadence (e.g., reviewed annually)
- Version control and approval signatures

A 2–4 page policy document is sufficient for non-ISO organisations.

### CSAT Process

Document **how** you collect and act on customer satisfaction feedback:

1. **When** surveys are sent (end of project, monthly, per milestone)
2. **How** surveys are sent (email, Microsoft Forms, third-party tool)
3. **Who** reviews results (Delivery Manager, Account Manager)
4. **How** low scores trigger action (escalation, account review)
5. **Where** results are stored and for how long

### CSAT Results

Provide a summary of CSAT results from the **last 12 months**. Anonymise customer names if required. The auditor wants to see:
- Overall average score
- Number of responses
- Trend over time (is quality improving?)
- Any outliers and how they were addressed

### Escalation Procedure

The escalation document should define:

| Level | Trigger | Owner | Response Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| Level 1 | Customer complaint raised | Delivery Lead | 4 hours |
| Level 2 | Unresolved after 24 hours | Account Manager | 24 hours |
| Level 3 | Client escalates to executive | CXO / MD | 48 hours |

## Common Gaps

| Gap | Remediation |
|---|---|
| No formal CSAT process | Set up a simple Microsoft Forms survey and document the process of sending it at project close |
| CSAT results exist but aren't aggregated | Export from your survey tool and create a summary Excel sheet |
| Escalation procedure is informal | Formalise the existing process into a 1-page document with names, triggers, and SLAs |
| No evidence of escalation process in action | Create a sanitised case study showing a complaint was received and resolved per the procedure |
